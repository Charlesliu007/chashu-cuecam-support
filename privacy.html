隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日 · 适用版本：V0.1（0.1.0）

本政策说明查叔题词 iPhone App（以下简称“本应用”）及其支持网页如何处理信息。应用服务由查叔题词开发者提供，隐私与支持联系邮箱为 charles@xneo.cn。

Read this policy in English

文案和参数保存在本机。开启语音跟随时，音频和稿件附近短语交由 Apple 语音识别处理；优先尝试设备端识别，不可用或失败时可能由 Apple 服务器处理。本应用不承诺所有语音识别均为离线处理。

1. 文案、拍摄偏好和录像

你编写的标题、文案、修改时间及拍摄与提词偏好存储于应用在本机的存储空间，用于保存稿件和恢复设置。本应用不设置用户账号，不向开发者服务器上传你的文案或录像。

相机画面与麦克风音频用于预览和录像。正常完成的录像写入系统“照片”；录制中或保存失败时，文件可能暂留在本机临时目录或系统提供的专业视频存储区域。设备备份、iCloud 照片同步及系统存储行为取决于你的系统设置和 Apple 服务规则。

2. 语音跟随和 Apple 服务

只有开启语音跟随时，本应用才会将麦克风音频以及当前稿件附近的短语交给 Apple Speech，以识别朗读并匹配提词进度。优先尝试设备端识别；设备端识别不可用、模型尚未就绪或识别出错时，可能改由系统选择的 Apple 服务处理，因此音频和上下文短语可能被发送至 Apple 服务器并产生网络流量。

识别文字用于当前会话的稿件匹配。本应用不将识别全文保存到文案库，也不把识别文字上传到开发者服务器。识别服务的可用性、处理位置及 Apple 对数据的保留与使用受其服务规则约束，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策及系统中的语音识别说明。

语音按钮变蓝表示跟随已开启；点击可关闭。退出拍摄界面会停止该提词会话。关闭语音跟随后仍可手动提词；录像中的声音采集由录像功能另行控制。请勿开启语音跟随朗读密码或不希望提交给语音服务的敏感内容。

3. 权限和设备信息

  • 相机：显示预览并录制视频。
  • 麦克风：录制声音；语音跟随开启时提供识别音频。
  • 语音识别：通过 Apple Speech 识别朗读、匹配稿件。
  • 照片添加：保存完成的录像；本应用不申请读取整个照片库。
  • 运动与方向信息：根据手机横持方向调整界面和提词位置。
  • 剩余存储容量和计时：估算可录时长、检查存储空间，并在空间不足时保护性停录。

这些设备信息用于本机功能。本应用不向开发者服务器发送运动数据、存储容量或系统启动时间。拒绝某项权限会限制相关功能，不代表必须授予全部权限才能编辑文案。你可以在系统设置中管理已授予的权限。

4. 广告、追踪和诊断

本应用没有广告 SDK、跨应用追踪、广告标识符收集或自建分析上传服务。设备上的诊断日志可能包含性能时长、格式、错误和设备能力信息，但应用不将文案或识别全文写入这些诊断日志，也不自行上传日志。Apple 的系统诊断或 App Store 服务按其自身政策和你的设置运行。

5. 支持邮件与网页访问

当你主动联系支持邮箱时，我们会收到你提供的邮箱地址、邮件正文、设备与问题描述，以及你附上的截图或其他文件，仅用于处理支持、隐私请求和必要的后续沟通。请先遮挡私人信息，并尽量不要发送完整私人稿件或未公开素材。

支持记录仅在处理请求和必要后续沟通所需期间保留；不再需要时删除，法律要求保留的情况除外。你可以通过同一邮箱请求访问、更正或删除你主动发送给我们的信息。我们不能通过邮件直接访问或修改你设备中的本地数据。

本支持网站通过 GitHub Pages 托管，为静态网页，没有登录、表单、广告或分析脚本，不读取 App 中的文案、录像或麦克风。GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址，并可能为交付页面及故障排查处理请求信息；这些技术处理与 App 本身的数据处理不同，请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。

6. 保留、删除和撤销权限

文案和偏好保留在本机，直至你删除相关文案、修改设置或卸载应用。可在文案列表中删除稿件，在“照片”中删除已保存视频。卸载应用通常移除本地文案和偏好，不会删除已存入“照片”的视频；系统备份中的副本须通过相应系统功能管理。

语音识别文字仅用于当前会话，不由应用持久化保存。关闭语音跟随可以停止继续向识别请求输入音频。Apple 服务端数据的保留、删除或其他权利请求应通过 Apple 提供的渠道处理，我们不承诺可以代为删除其服务端数据。

7. 数据分享与处理地点

本应用不会出售你的个人信息，也没有自建文案、音频或录像上传服务。语音识别、系统照片、备份和专业存储使用 Apple 提供的能力；你主动发送的支持邮件由收发邮件服务处理；网站请求由托管服务处理。服务处理位置可能不在你所在地区，具体取决于相关服务配置及政策。

8. 安全与未成年人

本应用利用系统权限和应用沙盒限制对本地内容的访问。请妥善保护设备，谨慎分享稿件和录像。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。本应用不以识别或收集未成年人的个人信息为目的；未成年人应在监护人指导下使用拍摄及语音功能，避免录入敏感信息。

9. 政策更新及联系

功能或数据处理发生变化时，我们会更新本页及适用的商店隐私说明，并注明更新日期。对于需要重新取得许可的变化，将按适用要求处理。隐私问题、数据请求或技术支持，请联系 charles@xneo.cn。

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: September 27, 2026. Applies to version 0.1.0.

This policy covers the 查叔题词 iPhone app and its support website. The app is provided by the 查叔题词 developer. Contact: charles@xneo.cn.

Scripts, settings and recordings

Script titles, text, modification dates and teleprompter/camera preferences are stored locally in the app. There is no user-account system and no developer-operated upload service for scripts or recordings. Camera images and microphone audio are used for preview and recording. Completed videos normally save to Photos; pending or failed saves may leave files in local temporary or system-managed professional video storage. Device backups and iCloud Photos depend on your Apple settings and policies.

Speech following

When voice following is enabled, microphone audio and nearby script phrases are passed to Apple Speech to recognize speech and align script progress. The app prioritizes on-device recognition; if unavailable or unsuccessful, the system-selected Apple service may process the request on Apple's servers. Audio and contextual phrases may therefore leave the device, and network access may be required. Recognition is not guaranteed to be offline.

Transcripts are used during the current session and are not persistently saved in the script library or uploaded to developer servers. Apple's processing, retention and availability follow its service policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy and your system's speech-recognition disclosures. A blue voice button indicates voice following is enabled; tap it to disable. Leaving the camera screen stops the session. Recording audio is controlled separately. Do not use voice following for passwords or other sensitive information you do not want processed by speech services.

Permissions, device data and diagnostics

Camera access enables preview and video capture. Microphone access supports recording and speech following. Speech permission enables Apple Speech. Add-only Photos permission saves videos without reading the entire library. Motion/orientation information adjusts the interface. Storage capacity and monotonic timing support remaining-time estimates and low-storage protection. Motion, capacity and boot-time information are not sent to developer servers. Denying a permission limits the corresponding feature; editing scripts does not require all permissions.

The app includes no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, advertising-identifier collection or developer-operated analytics upload service. Local diagnostics may contain performance, camera-format, capability and error information, but not script text or full transcripts. The app does not upload these logs. Apple's system diagnostics and App Store services are governed by their own settings and policies.

Support emails and website visits

If you email support, we receive the address, message, device/problem information and attachments you choose to send. We use them only to handle support or privacy requests and necessary follow-up. Remove private information from attachments. Support records are kept only while needed to resolve the request and necessary follow-up, unless legally required longer; you may email us to request access, correction or deletion. We cannot access local app data on your device remotely.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no login, forms, ads or analytics scripts and cannot read app scripts, videos or microphone audio. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and may process request information to deliver the website or investigate failures. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and third-party services

Local scripts and settings remain until deleted, changed or the app is uninstalled. Delete scripts in the app and saved videos in Photos. Uninstalling normally removes local app data but not videos in Photos; backup copies must be managed through the relevant system features. The app does not persist speech transcripts. Disabling voice following stops further audio input into the recognition request. Requests about Apple's server-side retention or deletion must be made through Apple's channels.

The app does not sell your personal information. Apple services handle speech recognition and the system's photo, backup and storage functions. Email providers handle support correspondence, and the hosting service handles website requests. Processing may occur outside your region according to the relevant service's configuration and policies.

Security, minors and updates

The app relies on system permissions and sandboxing to limit local access. Protect your device and share scripts or recordings carefully; no storage or transmission method is completely secure. The app is not intended to identify or collect children's personal information; minors should use camera and speech features under guardian guidance and avoid sensitive content.

We will update this page and applicable store disclosures when practices change, showing the update date and obtaining new permission where required. For privacy requests or support, email charles@xneo.cn.
